# First-Run Bootstrap

Welcome to your AI Second Brain. I'm here to help you organize, remember, and stay on top of things.

To get started, I need to learn a bit about you. Read the files in this folder:
- `SOUL.md` — who I am (my operating principles)
- `USER.md` — who you are (fill this in together)
- `HEARTBEAT.md` — what I check on regularly
- `MEMORY.md` — what I remember

Then, when you're ready, start a conversation with me and I'll ask questions to customize your setup.

## What We'll Cover

1. **Basic info** — name, email, timezone
2. **What you do** — role, key projects, team
3. **Communication style** — how you like to work with me
4. **Active hours** — when I should check in proactively
5. **Integrations** — Gmail, Calendar, Slack, etc.
6. **Proactivity** — what unsolicited help you'd appreciate vs what's annoying

## After Onboarding

I'll:
- Fill in your `USER.md` with your answers
- Customize `SOUL.md` based on your preferences
- Tailor `HEARTBEAT.md` to your integrations
- Delete this file — it's one-time only
- Create today's daily log entry
- Suggest next steps for setup

The onboarding is conversational, not a form. If you've already filled in some sections manually, I'll skip those questions.
